    A jewel of a scent

    The fragrance pulses with the sensual warmth of myrrh and labdanum, contrasted by the incisive coolness of sparkling aldehydes. This passionate amber heart is woven with earthy patchouli, creamy cedarwood, and precious French orris butter.

    Le Vestiaire des Parfums

    A YSL house legacy

    A tribute to Yves Saint Laurent’s iconic heart-shaped good-luck talisman, worn by his dearest muses from 1962 to 2002. Reimagined as a bold ambery spicy fragrance, CŒUR captures the jewel’s enduring spirit of devotion and daring.
